Every day, millions of footsteps fall on floors across the world, and all of that mechanical energy goes to waste. The Footstep Power Generation System puts it to use. Piezoelectric sensors embedded in the walking surface convert the pressure of each step into an electrical charge, which a power-conditioning circuit rectifies, regulates, and stores for use. An Arduino Uno/Nano monitors footsteps and key electrical parameters such as voltage, giving real-time insight into the energy generated. The system can power low-energy loads like LEDs and small electronic devices, and it is a promising step toward sustainable, self-powered infrastructure in crowded places such as railway stations, malls, and school corridors.
